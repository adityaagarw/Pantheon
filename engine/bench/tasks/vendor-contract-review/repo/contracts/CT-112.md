# Software License and Services Agreement

**CT-112**. Meridian Software, Inc. ("Meridian") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer").

**1. Term.** Sixty (60) months from January 1, 2026, ending at the end of that period with no
automatic renewal.

**2. Termination for convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for
convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice.

**3. Fees.** Customer will pay license and support fees of USD 310,000 per year.

**4. Limitation of liability.** Meridian's total liability shall not exceed the greater of
(a) USD 500,000 and (b) the fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months
preceding the claim.

**5. Data protection.** Meridian processes Customer personal data under the Data Processing
Agreement executed with and attached to this Agreement as Exhibit D.

**6. Governing law.** The laws of the State of Delaware govern this Agreement.
