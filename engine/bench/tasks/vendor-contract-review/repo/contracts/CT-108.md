# Enterprise Connectivity Agreement

CT-108 · Orbit Telecom Ltd (Dublin) ("Orbit") · Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer")

**2.1 Initial Term.** Thirty-six (36) months starting 1 September 2024.

**2.2 Renewal.** Thereafter the Agreement renews for consecutive twelve (12) month periods
unless the Customer notifies Orbit of non-renewal at least one hundred and eighty (180)
days before the end of the current period.

**2.3 Orbit's right to terminate.** Orbit may terminate this Agreement for convenience on
ninety (90) days' notice. (For the avoidance of doubt, this right is Orbit's only.)

**3. Charges.** USD 72,000 per year. Renewal charges may increase by up to 5%.

**4. Liability.** Orbit's aggregate liability in any contract year is limited to
USD 50,000.

**5. Data.** Orbit carries Customer's traffic but does not process personal data on
Customer's behalf.

**6. Governing law.** The laws of Ireland govern this Agreement.
