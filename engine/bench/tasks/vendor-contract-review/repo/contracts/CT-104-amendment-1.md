# Amendment No. 1 to Logistics Framework Agreement CT-104

Dated 10 September 2025, between Rhein Logistik AG and Halcyon Labs Inc.

The parties agree the following changes. All other terms remain unchanged.

1. Clause 1 is amended so that the initial term is thirty (30) months (instead of 24), and
   notice of non-renewal must be given at least ninety (90) days before the expiry of the
   current period (instead of 120 days).
2. Clause 4 is replaced by: "The Carrier's aggregate liability under this agreement is
   limited to EUR 100,000."
