# Facility Services Agreement

CT-109 · Vogel Facility Services GmbH ("Vogel") · Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer")

- **Start:** 1 January 2023, initial term thirty-six (36) months, then automatically
  renewed for one (1) year at a time unless either party gives ninety (90) days' notice.
- **Fees:** EUR 96,000 per year; renewal increases limited to 3%.
- **Data:** Vogel processes visitor log data (personal data) under the signed DPA in
  Annex 1.
- **Liability:** limited to the annual fees (1x).
- **Law:** German law.
