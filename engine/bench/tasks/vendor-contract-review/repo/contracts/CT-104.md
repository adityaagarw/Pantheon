# Logistics Framework Agreement

**CT-104.** Rhein Logistik AG ("Carrier") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer").

## Clause 1: Duration
The framework agreement starts on 1 October 2024 and has a fixed initial term of 24 months.
It then renews automatically for further periods of 24 months unless notice of non-renewal is
given at least 120 days before the expiry of the current period.

## Clause 2: Remuneration
The annual framework fee is EUR 260,000. Upon renewal, the framework fee may rise by at most
3 % per renewal.

## Clause 3: Personal data
The Carrier processes names and delivery addresses of Customer's end customers. The data
processing agreement in Annex B (signed) is part of this agreement.

## Clause 4: Liability
The Carrier's liability per year is capped at one and a half (1.5) times the annual
framework fee.

## Clause 5: Governing law
This agreement is governed by the law of the Federal Republic of Germany.
