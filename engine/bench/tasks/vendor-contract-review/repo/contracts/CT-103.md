# Liefervertrag / Supply Agreement: Office Paper and Consumables

Contract no. CT-103 between Papier & Co GmbH, Köln ("Lieferant") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer").

1. **Commencement.** This supply agreement begins on 1 July 2023 and runs for an initial
   period of one (1) year.
2. **Extension.** The agreement is extended automatically by one (1) year at a time unless
   terminated with three (3) months' notice to the end of the respective contract year.
3. **Price.** The flat annual price for the agreed volumes is EUR 38,500 (net). On each
   extension, prices are adjusted to the Lieferant's then-current list prices.
4. **Liability.** The Lieferant's liability for slight negligence is limited to
   EUR 25,000 per contract year in aggregate.
5. **Data.** The Lieferant does not process personal data on behalf of the Customer;
   delivery is to the Customer's office reception.
6. **Applicable law.** German law applies. Place of jurisdiction is Köln.
