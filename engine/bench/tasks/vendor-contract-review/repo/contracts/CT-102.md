# Master Services Agreement: Contingent Staffing

**Contract CT-102.** Parties: Brightline Staffing Inc. ("Supplier") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer").

**Effective date:** January 15, 2025. **Term:** thirty-six (36) months from the effective
date. The agreement expires at the end of the term; any extension requires a written
amendment signed by both parties.

**Charges.** Supplier's charges are estimated at USD 420,000 per contract year, invoiced
monthly in arrears based on hours worked.

**Personnel data.** To administer timesheets and payroll for contractors, Supplier will
receive and process personal data of Customer's employees and managers (names, work
emails, cost centres). The parties have not executed a separate data processing agreement.

**Limitation of liability.** Except for fraud, Supplier's aggregate liability under this
Agreement is limited to two (2) times the annual charges.

**Termination for convenience.** Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any
reason or no reason, on sixty (60) days' written notice to Supplier.

**Law and venue.** New York law governs this Agreement. The parties submit to the courts of
New York County.
