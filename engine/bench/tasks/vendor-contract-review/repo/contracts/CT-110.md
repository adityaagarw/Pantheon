# Payroll Services Agreement

**CT-110** — Northstar Payroll Corp. ("Northstar") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer")

**Term.** The agreement begins June 1, 2024 for an initial term of two (2) years and renews
automatically for additional one (1) year terms unless a party provides written notice of
non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

**Fees.** USD 12,500 per month. Northstar may increase fees on renewal by no more than five
percent (5%).

**Employee data.** Northstar will process personal data of Customer's employees (including
bank details and tax identifiers) to run payroll. The parties will negotiate in good faith
and use reasonable efforts to agree a data processing agreement within ninety (90) days of
the Effective Date.

**Limitation of liability.** Northstar's liability is limited to the fees paid or payable
in the twelve (12) months before the claim arose.

**Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
