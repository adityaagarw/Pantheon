# Translation Services Agreement

CT-111 · Quill Translation Pte. Ltd. ("Quill") · Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer")

1. **Term.** 12 months from 1 September 2025, renewing automatically for further 12-month
   terms unless either party gives thirty (30) days' notice before the end of a term.
2. **Fees.** EUR 22,000 per year. On renewal, fees may increase by the change in the
   Singapore Consumer Price Index plus two percent (2%).
3. **Liability.** Quill's liability shall not exceed the annual fees (one times).
4. **Personal data.** Quill translates product documentation only and does not process
   personal data.
5. **Governing law.** The laws of the Republic of Singapore.
