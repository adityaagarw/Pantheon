# Data Platform Services Agreement

Contract reference **CT-106**. Lumen Analytics Ltd, London ("Lumen") and Halcyon Labs Inc. ("Customer").

1. **Term.** This Agreement starts on 1 February 2026 and continues for forty-eight (48)
   months. It ends automatically at the end of that period.
2. **Fees.** GBP 120,000 per annum, payable quarterly in advance.
3. **Termination.** Either party may terminate for the other's insolvency or uncured
   material breach. There is no right to terminate for convenience.
4. **Liability cap.** Lumen's maximum aggregate liability shall be GBP 50,000.
5. **Data protection.** Lumen processes Customer's customer data, including personal
   data, as processor under the Data Processing Agreement in Schedule 2, executed with this
   Agreement.
6. **Governing law.** This Agreement and any non-contractual obligations arising out of it
   are governed by the law of England and Wales.
