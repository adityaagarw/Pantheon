## Services Pantheon connects to

Pantheon talks to these over the network but doesn't include them: LLM providers you configure
(OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama, vLLM, llama.cpp or
LM Studio), MCP servers you add, and [audio.cpp](https://github.com/0xShug0/audio.cpp) for voice. Their
terms, and the licenses of any models you run, apply to your use of them.
